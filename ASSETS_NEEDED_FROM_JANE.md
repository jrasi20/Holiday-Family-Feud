## 1. Music from Pixabay (pixabay.com/music)

Pixabay's license allows commercial use without attribution. For each track, check that the page says "Free for use under the Pixabay Content License", and **skip any track tagged "Content ID"** (those can cause YouTube claims on your promo videos).

Save into `docs\assets\music\`:

| Save as | What it's for | Search Pixabay for | What to pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| `menu.mp3` | Cover + lobby loop | `christmas upbeat`, `christmas jingle`, `holiday party` | Cheerful, sleigh bells, about 1–2 min, loops cleanly. Instrumental only. |
| `game.mp3` | Quiet bed under gameplay | `christmas background`, `christmas lofi`, `gentle christmas piano` | Calm, NOT busy, no big melody. It plays under the timer and dings for minutes. |
| `win.mp3` | Final winner screen | `christmas fanfare`, `victory orchestral`, `celebration bells` | Short and triumphant, 10–30 s, ending on a big chord. |
| `round.mp3` *(optional)* | "ROUND 3 · DOUBLE POINTS" sting | `game show intro`, `drum roll`, `stinger` | 2–4 s punchy hit. |
| `fastmoney.mp3` *(optional)* | Fast Money bonus round | `ticking tension`, `countdown`, `quiz show` | Ticking, fast, energetic, 30 s+ that loops. |

Avoid recordings of copyrighted songs (Mariah Carey, "Feliz Navidad" and so on). Classic carols (Jingle Bells, Deck the Halls, We Wish You, Joy to the World, O Come All Ye Faithful) are public domain, so a Pixabay recording of one of those is fine.
